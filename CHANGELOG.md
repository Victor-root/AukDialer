# 📋 Changelog

## 🚀 v0.9.1-alpha (2026-11-25)

A side menu to choose instead of the bottom bar, neutral bars and faster pages.

### ✨ Added
- **Side menu**: the tabs can move to a menu that opens from the left, instead of the bottom bar. It also leads to blocked numbers, private contacts, speed dial, backup, screenshot mode and settings. Optionally, it opens with a swipe from the first tab.
- **Neutral bars**: the header, status bar and navigation bar in white or black instead of the accent color.

### 🔄 Changed
- **The contact page opens smoothly** and shows the contact's name in the header.
- **Call recordings show up at once**, like voicemail.
- **The missed call notification names the SIM** only when the phone has several.

### 🐛 Fixed
- **No more notification for calls blocked by a spam blocking app** (Saracroche, for instance): that app decides whether to tell you.
- Various small fixes.

## 🚀 v0.9.0-alpha (2026-11-21)

The first public release of AukDialer: a modern, free and open-source Phone app for Android, with no ads, no tracking and no Google services.

### ✨ Highlights
- **Customizable call screen**: animated, plain, gradient or photo backgrounds, and four ways to answer.
- **Call recording** through Shizuku.
- **T9 search**, filterable call history, encrypted private contacts and call blocking.
- **Customizable from end to end**: Material You colors or 20 colors of your choice, and the app icon on your home screen can take the same color. AMOLED mode, 11 avatar shapes, Material 3 Expressive design.
- **Edge to edge mode**, optional: the app draws under the status and navigation bars, and the header folds away as you scroll.
- **Real contact management**: addresses, birthdays and other dates, kept on the phone or synced with your Google account, and shown in your own date format.
- **Screenshot mode**: hides names, numbers and photos, so a screenshot of a bug can be shared as is.
- **Visual voicemail** (experimental): only tested with one French carrier so far. If it doesn't work with yours, copy the diagnostics from Settings > Voicemail and attach them to an issue.
